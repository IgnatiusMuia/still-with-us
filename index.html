About

A practice built on listening.

I'm Margaret. I've been reading for people for twenty-two years. The last nine have been in other people's front rooms and on video; before that, a small office above a bookshop on Warren Street, with a radiator that knocked and stairs too steep for anyone with a bad knee.

I come to you, or we meet by video. Your home, your quiet, or a room of your own with the screen between us. I've sat in farmhouses in Columbia County, studio apartments in Hudson, and kitchens in three time zones. I've moved a cat off the good chair more times than I can count. I bring my own cushion because most dining chairs are too hard for an hour, and I take my shoes off at the door unless you tell me not to.

I don't need photographs, jewelry, or anything of your person's. You don't have to believe anything in particular. Most people who sit with me are carrying something they haven't found a place to put. That's enough to start.

I won't invent a message to fill a silence. If I get nothing, I'll say so. The people we've lost don't stop being part of the room. That's the work, and it's why I meet you in your home or on a screen rather than in an office.

Still With Us is just me, a paper diary, a car that knows the back roads, and a laptop for the video sessions. I keep the client list small on purpose. It's easier to be honest when I'm not rushing to the next appointment.

If you're wondering whether this is for you, read the FAQ first. For direct messages, I'm on Telegram — @ConnectStillWithUs.

Signed, Margaret

Margaret Ellery · Hudson, New York

Questions

Before you book.

If yours isn't here, message me on Telegram. I answer direct messages myself, usually within a day.

Why is this more expensive?

It isn't a chat. A 90-minute reading takes about four hours of my day once you count the drive or the video setup, the 30 to 45 minutes of preparation, the follow-up note, and the admin that keeps the practice running. I don't upsell recordings or sell you a package you didn't ask for. The price on the page is the price.

Do I really pay before?

Yes. Payment holds the time. I've had too many no-shows to work any other way. You'll see the full amount and any travel fee before you pay. Payment is by Skrill or Neteller, and every booking gets a receipt.

What if I cancel?

Cancel 72 or more hours ahead and you get a full refund minus a 10% admin fee. Inside 72 hours, the session is non-refundable, but I'll reschedule you once within 60 days. If something serious has happened, talk to me. I'm not a computer.

Do you travel outside 25 miles?

Yes, for about $2 per mile beyond 25 miles from Hudson. I'll confirm the distance before anything is charged. If you're far out, message me first. I'd rather quote you honestly than surprise you at checkout.

Is my info private?

Yes. I keep notes in a locked drawer, I don't record sessions, and your name doesn't go on a mailing list. If I see you in town, I won't bring up what we talked about. That's your story to tell.

What should I have ready on the day?

A quiet room with two chairs and a glass of water for each of us. Put your devices on silent. For a video session, find a steady place for your screen and make sure the room is private. You don't need photographs, jewelry, or anything of your person's. If you'd like a photo nearby, that's fine. Wear something you can sit in for an hour. I run cold, so I'll probably ask to sit away from the draft.

Can I book for someone else?

Only if they know and want it. No surprise readings. Grief isn't a gift you can wrap.

Can I choose online or in person?

Yes — every session is offered both ways. We agree the details on Telegram.

How do I get the video link?

On Telegram, before your session.

How do I contact you?

Telegram — @ConnectStillWithUs. It's the only direct channel I use.
